# Welcome on Red Fox blog ! 


My name is Dorian, and I am passionate about technology and open-source philosophy. On this blog, I enjoy exploring unconventional topics and sharing tips that you won’t always find in official documentation.

Here, curiosity is at the heart of everything we do. I love experimenting, testing new ideas, and sharing these discoveries with you. Whether it's exploring new technologies or revisiting old concepts from a different perspective, this blog is a space dedicated to innovation and experimentation.

I invite you to join this journey of technological exploration, where each post is an opportunity to discover something new. Thank you for being part of this enriching adventure on the Red Fox Blog.
